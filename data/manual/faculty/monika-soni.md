# Faculty Profile: Dr. Monika Soni

- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Organizational Behaviour & HR
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/monika-soni
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Human Resource Management Contemporary Issues in HR Principles of Management Individual and Organizational Dynamics Data Visualization with Tableau
- **Research Interests**: Leadership Corporate Social Responsibility Green Behaviour at Workplace Artificial Intelligence in Human Resource Management
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Monika Soni holds a Ph.D. in Carbon Management from Mohanlal Sukhadia University. With a stellar academic record as a gold medalist in both BBM and MBA, she has been an Assistant Professor in the OB/HR area, teaching courses such as Human Resource Management and Organisational Behaviour at reputed institutions. Her research expertise lies in Human Resource Management and Organisational Behavior, with a focus on transformational leadership and pro-environmental practices in organizations. Her work has been published in prestigious ABDC-category journals, and she has presented at leading national and international conferences. Recognized for her contributions, she has completed the Faculty Development Programme at IIM Ahmedabad, an HR Analytics certification from IIM Rohtak, and a certification in SMART PLS 4 software.
