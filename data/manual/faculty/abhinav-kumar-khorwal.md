# Faculty Profile: Dr. Abhinav Kumar Khorwal

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Abhinav Kumar Khorwal
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/abhinav-kumar-khorwal
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Undergraduate
- **Research Interests**: Magnetic materials Phase transition Frustrated magnetism Spin glasses Magnetocaloric effect
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Abhinav holds a PhD from Central University of Rajasthan, Ajmer and Postdoctoral Research from Indian Institute of Technology, Madras, in the area of Experimental Condensed Matter Physics with focus on Magnetic materials, Frustrated magnetism, Magnetic phase transition and Magnetocaloric effect (MCE). His research expertise is anchored around Spin glasses, Critical phenomena and MCE. His research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals like Journal of Alloys and Compounds and Journal of Magnetism and Magnetic Materials. His research and efforts have been recognised through ‘Best Poster Award’ by ISMS-2024’ by CURAJ.
