# Faculty Profile: Afreen Gani Faridi

- **Faculty Name**: Afreen Gani Faridi
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Centre for Communication and Critical Thinking (CCCT)
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/afreen-gani-faridi
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Critical Thinking and Storytelling Perspectives on Contemporary Issues Law, Technology and Society Basic of the Indian Constitution and the State Communication and Identity Critical Thinking for Decisions at Workplace
- **Research Interests**: Ecologies of Labour Constitutionalism Decolonisation Pastoralism Literature and Cultural Hegemonies
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Afreen is an Assistant Professor at the Centre for Communication and Critical Thinking, JK Lakshmipat University. Afreen is a recipient of the ICSSR Doctoral Fellowship and MCRG- Rosa Luxemburg Grant for research on pastoralism. He has been awarded a Gold Medal for his Master’s in Public Administration and has qualified UGC-NET in Political Science. Afreen is a founding member and lead organiser of the Peoples of Himalayas Academic Discourse Network and a member of the Ecologies of Labour network. His interests lie in utilising Political-Economy to analyse shifts in Labour Practices and Mobility amongst nomadic communities in the Western Himalayas. He aims to decolonise epistemes and pedagogies and empower academic scholars belonging to the Western Himalayas. His work on Covid, nomads and mobility has been published in edited volumes by Routledge and Lexington.
