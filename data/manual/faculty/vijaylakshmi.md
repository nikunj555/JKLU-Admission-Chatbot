# Faculty Profile: Dr. Vijaylakshmi

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Vijaylakshmi
- **Designation**: Associate Professor and Co-ordinator
- **Department**: Centre for Communication and Critical Thinking (CCCT)
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/vijaylakshmi
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Fundamentals of Communication Critical Thinking & Storytelling Communication and Identity Understanding & Managing Conflict Critical Thinking for Decisions at Workplace Business Communication
- **Research Interests**: English Literature English Language Teaching Critical Thinking
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr Vijaylakshmi holds a PhD from Nirwan University, Jaipur in 2024 in the area of teaching English Language to students through English Literature. Her other PhD is in 2013 that talks about the Trojan War and the Characterisation of Great Alexander. In her academic career spanning around 2 decades, she has taught English literature, Accent neutralisation, English language, Life skills, and Critical thinking at Government and private universities of good repute. Her research expertise is anchored around Greek literature and English Language Teaching (ELT). Her research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals. Her research and teaching have been recognised through ‘Star Award’ by Satr iEngineering. She is currently the Team Leader for Cambridge Assessment English for hiring and training Speaking Examiners.
