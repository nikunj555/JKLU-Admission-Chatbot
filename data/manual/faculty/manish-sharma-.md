# Faculty Profile: Manish Sharma

- **Faculty Name**: Manish Sharma
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Institute of Design
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Design
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/manish-sharma-
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Visual Representation Form Studies Material Explorations Simple Product Design Elements of Design Teaching Interest Product Innovation Digital Interfaces Form and Spaces Systems Thinking Art, Culture & Society
- **Research Interests**: Automobile Design Trend Forecasting User Experience Design Sustainable Materials
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Manish Sharma holds a Master of Design degree from IDC School of Design, IIT Bombay, specializing in Mobility and Vehicle Design. He also holds a Bachelor of Design (B.Des) degree from NID Ahmedabad, two of India’s most prestigious design institutions. With a deep passion for 2-wheelers and expertise in automobile design, Manish has contributed to innovative projects, including integrating AI in car experiences with Gensol EV and working on conceptual designs with TVS Motors. In his academic career, he has taught Visual Representation, Material Exploration, Elements of Design, Space and Form Studies etc. emphasizing hands-on learning, fostering creativity, and exploring the interplay between materials and technology.
