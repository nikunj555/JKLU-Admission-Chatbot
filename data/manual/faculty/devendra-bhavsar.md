# Faculty Profile: Devendra Bhavsar

- **Faculty Name**: Devendra Bhavsar
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Computer Science and Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/devendra-bhavsar
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Object Oriented Programming Data Structures and Algorithm Cyber Security Computer Networks Programming I (C Programming) Programming I (JAVA Programming) Database Management Systems Operating Systems
- **Research Interests**: Wireless Sensor Networks and Algorithms Edge Computing Performance Benchmarking Service Operations
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Mr. Devendra Bhavsar is Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science Engineering. He did his M. Tech. in Computer Science & Engineering from IIT Bombay in 2009, and B.E. in Computer Science from Ujjain Engineering College Ujjain in 2006. He has over 15 years of academic experience. He is pursuing his Ph.D. in the area of Big Data Analytics. He is CISCO Certified Academy Instructor (CCAI) from CISCO. He has published several research papers in refereed International and National journals (ESCI/Scopus indexed/Web of science/UGC indexed) and conference proceedings. During his academic career, he has held various administrative positions such as Academic Coordinator IT, CISCO Networking Academy In-charge, Chief Warden, member of Anti-ragging committee, member of Library committee Sports Club In-charge etc. He has actively participated in various conferences and workshops. He has appointed as paper setter and an external examiner for the viva-voce exams of B.Tech/M.Tech students at various universities. Prior to joining JK Lakshmipat University, Jaipur he was associated with Govt. Ujjain Engineering College Ujjain and ICFAI University Dehradun. Read More
