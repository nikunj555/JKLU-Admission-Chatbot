# Faculty Profile: Dr. Satveer Singh

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Satveer Singh
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Computer Science and Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/satveer-singh
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Data Structures and Algorithms Computer Networks Python Programming Cloud and Edge Computing
- **Research Interests**: Information verified via official faculty directory
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr Satveer Singh holds a PhD from Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi, in the area of Fog-integrated Cloud computing with focus on Fog Device Deployment and IoT Workload Distribution. In his academic career spanning more than 4 years, he has taught Data Structures and Algorithms, Computer Networks, Theory of Computation, Compiler Design, and Cloud and Edge Computing at Engineering institutions in India. His research expertise is anchored around optimal deployment of computing devices and user requests using metaheuristic approaches. His research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals. He has cleared GATE exam for seven times and cleared UGC-JRF entrance test two times. He is currently working as an Assistant Professor in the Institute of Engineering & Technology at JK Lakshmipat University, Jaipur.
