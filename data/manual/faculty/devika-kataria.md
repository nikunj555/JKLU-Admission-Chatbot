# Faculty Profile: Dr. Devika Kataria

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Devika Kataria
- **Designation**: Professor
- **Department**: Electronics & Communication Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/devika-kataria
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Analog VLSI Circuits Digital Systems Design Internet of Things Principles of Cyber Security
- **Research Interests**: Cyber Physical Systems Communication Networks Flexible Electronics
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Devika Kataria earned her Ph.D. in Microelectronics and VLSI from IIT Kanpur. She holds a B.E. and M.Tech. in Electronics and Digital Communication from MANIT, Bhopal. With nine years of experience in the optical fiber industry, she played a pivotal role in establishing an optical fiber manufacturing plant, leading it to achieve ISO certification. In academia, Dr. Kataria was honored as the Best Tutor at IIT Kanpur in 2013 for Fundamentals for Electronics and received good feedback consistently for the course. She is deeply committed to mentoring students on projects and internships in Cyber-Physical Systems and Cybersecurity. Her guidance has also extended to fostering innovation in sustainable development and the circular economy, where her teams have secured international incubation grants through the Circular Cities Enterprise Program.
