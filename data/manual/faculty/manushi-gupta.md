# Faculty Profile: Dr. Manushi Gupta

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Manushi Gupta
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Mathematics
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/manushi-gupta
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Linear Algebra and Differential Equations Number Theory Calculus Discrete Mathematics
- **Research Interests**: Mathematical Modelling Partial Differential Equations Generalized Thermoelasticity Theory Non-Fourier Heat Conduction Problem
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr . Manushi Gupta completed her PhD under Prof. Santwana Mukhopadhyay at IIT (BHU), Varanasi. She holds a B.Sc. (Hons.) and M.Sc. in Mathematics from Delhi University. As an accomplished researcher, she has published five papers as a first author and co-authored three others, focusing on Mathematical Modelling of Coupled Thermomechanical Problems. Manushi has also worked as an online tutor and subject matter expert at Trivium Education Services. She believes in composite learning and thinks of teaching as a mutual process of knowledge exchange between students and teachers. She sees herself as a competent faculty member and an aspiring researcher in future.
