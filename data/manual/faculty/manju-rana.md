# Faculty Profile: Dr. Manju Rana

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Manju Rana
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Electronics & Communication Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/manju-rana
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Internet of Things Data Communication and Networks Digital Electronics Database Management Systems Signals and Systems Digital Signal Processing Embedded Systems Elements of AI and Machine Learning Wireless Communication Data Visualization Distributed Systems Operating Systems Fundamentals of Electrical and Electronics Engineering
- **Research Interests**: IoT Wearable Devices Motion Analytics Data-based Kinematic Profiling of Athletes
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Manju Rana is a research-focused academic and technology professional with over 10 years of combined experience across academia, research, industry, and entrepreneurship. She completed her Ph.D. in Electronics & Communication Engineering from NIT Kurukshetra in 2025, following an M.Tech. in Information & Communication Technology from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University and a B.Tech. from Banasthali University. Dr. Rana has established a strong research profile with multiple SCIE-indexed journal publications and IEEE conference papers in wearable sensor technology, sports performance analytics, and machine learning. She was recognized among the Top 20 Innovations at GITEX Technology Week 2016 and has qualified GATE in both 2014 and 2016. Her unique blend of academic excellence, research expertise, industry exposure, and leadership in emerging technologies makes her a well-rounded educator and researcher in the field of engineering and data-driven sports analytics.
