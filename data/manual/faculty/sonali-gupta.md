- **Biography & Academic Background**: With a strong foundation in design education and practice across India and the United Kingdom, Sonali Gupta brings a multidisciplinary perspective to the classroom. She holds a Master of Design (M.Des) from the School of Art and Design, Nottingham Trent University, England and a Bachelor of Design (B.Des) from the Symbiosis Institute of Design, Pune, India. With experience in both academic leadership and creative practice, she is passionate about exploring how design thinking and research can drive innovation, empathy, and impact in diverse contexts. Her approach to teaching blends conceptual exploration with practical application, encouraging students to think critically, prototype boldly, and design responsibly.
