# Faculty Profile: Aman Mishra

- **Faculty Name**: Aman Mishra
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Centre for Communication and Critical Thinking (CCCT)
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/aman-mishra
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Indian Knowledge System The Study of the Mahabharata History of Ancient and Medieval India
- **Research Interests**: Early Medieval South Asian Economic History Urban History Social and Political History History and Culture of Rajasthan
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Mr. Aman Mishra possesses an impressive academic background in History. He is currently in the final stage of his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mandi. He completed his M.A. in History from the University of Rajasthan and B.A. (Hons.) in History from Rajasthan College, University of Rajasthan. In addition, he has pursued specialized certification courses in Basic Brāhmī Script Writing & Deciphering and Basic Sanskrit from reputed institutions. A dedicated scholar and researcher, he has contributed significantly through research publications, conference presentations, workshops, and academic collaborations at national and international platforms. His passion for research, interdisciplinary learning, and student engagement certainly adds immense value to the academic community in education.
