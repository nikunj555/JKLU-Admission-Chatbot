# JKLU Residential Life: Hostel Room Facilities, Services & Allotment Requirements

Comprehensive guide to residential hostel infrastructure, daily amenities, student services, and enrollment requirements at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU).

## 1. Hostel Room Facilities & Amenities
Every residential hostel room at JKLU is thoughtfully furnished and ergonomically designed to provide a comfortable living and studying environment:
- **Bed & Mattress**: Sturdy individual bed frame with comfortable mattress for each resident student.
- **Study Table**: Dedicated individual study desk with ample surface area for laptops, books, and study lamps.
- **Ergonomic Chair**: Comfortable study chair designed for extended academic sessions.
- **Spacious Wardrobe / Almirah**: Individual lockable wardrobe/almirah with shelf compartments and hanging rods for clothes and personal belongings.
- **Continuous Electricity Supply**: Reliable 24/7 power supply backed by heavy-duty institutional generators to ensure uninterrupted electricity during outages.
- **High-Speed Wi-Fi Connectivity**: High-bandwidth campus Wi-Fi access points strategically installed throughout hostel corridors and wings for seamless internet connectivity.
- **Air Conditioning / Climate Options**: Availability of both Air-Conditioned (AC) and well-ventilated Non-AC accommodation options across single, double, and quad sharing layouts.
- **Common Areas**: Vibrant common lounges, recreational TV rooms, indoor games spaces (table tennis, carrom, chess), and communal reading lobbies.

---

## 2. Comprehensive Hostel Services
The university provides an extensive ecosystem of student-first residential services:
- **Daily Housekeeping**: Regular cleaning and sanitation of student corridors, washrooms, shared bathrooms, and common facilities by dedicated custodial staff.
- **In-House Laundry Facility**: Professional on-campus mechanized laundry and ironing services with convenient drop-off and pickup schedules.
- **Safe Drinking Water**: Commercial Reverse Osmosis (RO) water purification plants with hot, cold, and ambient water dispensing units installed on every floor.
- **Mess & Dining Facility**: Spacious, hygienic student dining mess serving four nutritious meals daily (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner) overseen by student mess committees.
- **Round-the-Clock Security**: Trained security guards stationed 24/7 at all hostel entry and exit gates.
- **CCTV Surveillance**: Continuous closed-circuit television (CCTV) electronic surveillance in common corridors, building entrances, and perimeter perimeters for enhanced safety.
- **Maintenance Support**: Prompt on-campus electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and air-conditioning maintenance team to resolve room repair requests logged via student portal.
- **Warden & Residence Support**: Experienced resident Wardens, assistant wardens, and faculty residential advisors living on campus to guide students, manage hostel discipline, and provide emergency pastoral care.

---

## 3. Hostel Allotment Requirements & Procedures
Students opting for campus accommodation must complete the official residential registration process:
- **Hostel Application Form**: Duly filled and submitted hostel accommodation request form indicating room type preference.
- **Hostel Allotment Confirmation**: Official room allotment letter/slip issued by the Chief Warden / Hostel Administration.
- **Valid University Student ID**: Official JKLU student identity card or provisional enrollment slip for identity verification.
- **Parent / Guardian Information**: Complete, verified contact details, residential address proof, and emergency contact numbers of parents or authorized local guardians.
- **Hostel Undertakings & Declarations**: Signed acceptance of the official JKLU Hostel Rules and Code of Conduct, including curfew timings, visitor protocols, and substance prohibition.
- **Emergency Medical Contact Details**: Detailed record of resident blood group, pre-existing medical conditions (if any), and designated primary and secondary emergency contacts.
