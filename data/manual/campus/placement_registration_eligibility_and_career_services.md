# JKLU Placement Guidelines: Registration Documents, Eligibility Criteria, Preparation & Career Profiles

Official guidelines and operational protocols for campus placements, recruitment eligibility, student career profiles, and preparation at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU).

## 1. Placement Registration Documents
Students seeking campus placement opportunities through the university Placement Cell must register and submit the following verified documentation:

### Personal Information
- **University Email ID**: Student's active, official university email address for placement communications, test links, and interview schedules.
- **Mobile Number**: Student's active primary mobile contact number for urgent WhatsApp updates and recruitment calls.
- **Enrollment / Registration Number**: Official university enrollment number issued by the Registrar.
- **Photograph**: Recent professional passport-size color photograph for candidate profile dossiers and identity badges.
- **Government Photo ID**: Valid government-issued photo identity proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
- **Residential Address**: Updated permanent and current correspondence residential address records.

### Academic Documents
- **Class 10 Marksheet & Passing Certificate**: Official secondary school examination marksheet and certificate.
- **Class 12 Marksheet & Passing Certificate**: Official senior secondary qualifying examination marksheet and certificate.
- **Semester Marksheets**: Marksheets/grade cards for all completed university semesters.
- **CGPA / SGPA Records**: Latest official grade transcript validating cumulative and semester performance.
- **Degree / Provisional Certificate**: Degree certificate or provisional degree certificate (for postgraduate or graduating students, if applicable).

### Placement Documents & Portfolio
- **Resume / Curriculum Vitae (CV)**: Standardized, updated, and Placement Cell-approved resume.
- **Internship Certificates & Experience Letters**: Verification letters, completion certificates, and project performance appraisals from prior summer/industrial internships.
- **Project Certificates**: Documentation, live repository links, and evaluation certificates for academic capstone, minor, or independent technical projects.
- **Technical & Online Course Certifications**: Industry-recognized certifications (e.g. AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Cisco, Coursera, NPTEL, edX) and specialized skill credentials.
- **Achievement & Extracurricular Certificates**: Awards, hackathon citations, collegiate competition honors, and student leadership certificates.

### Placement Registration Declarations
- **Placement Registration Form**: Completed official annual placement registration form.
- **Student Placement Profile**: Comprehensive candidate dossier including career objectives, verified academic scores, and contact information.
- **Role & Location Preferences**: Explicit selection of preferred job profiles and geographic work locations.
- **Skills Inventory**: Documented technical programming skills, frameworks, tools, and professional language/communication proficiencies.
- **Eligibility Declaration**: Signed student undertaking declaring accuracy of submitted credentials and adherence to recruitment codes.
- **Data Sharing Consent**: Written consent authorizing the Placement Cell to share the candidate's resume, academic history, and contact details with visiting corporate recruiters.

## 2. Placement Eligibility Criteria

### Academic Eligibility
- **Minimum CGPA Requirement**: Candidate must satisfy the specific cut-off CGPA mandated by individual recruiting companies (commonly 6.0, 6.5, 7.0, or 7.5+).
- **Backlog Criteria**: No active/standing academic backlogs at the time of recruitment, unless specifically permitted by the recruiter.
- **Previous Backlog Clearance**: All historical backlogs must have been cleared in prior supplementary/re-evaluation examinations.
- **Secondary & Higher Secondary Cut-offs**: Fulfillment of company-specific minimum percentage requirements in Class 10 and Class 12 (often 60% or 65%+ aggregate).
- **Graduation Benchmark**: Minimum graduation/programme CGPA required at the time of final onboarding.

### Recruitment Process Eligibility
- **Mandatory Registration**: Student must be formally registered and in good standing with the university Placement Cell.
- **Company-Specific Benchmarks**: Candidate must meet all profile-specific prerequisites stipulated in the recruiter's Job Description (JD).
- **Deadline Compliance**: Strictly adhere to all application deadlines, resume submission windows, and form submissions.
- **Pre-Placement Talks (PPT)**: Mandatory attendance at scheduled corporate pre-placement presentations and company introductory sessions.
- **Test & Interview Attendance**: Mandatory punctual appearance for all online/offline assessments, technical coding rounds, and interview calls once shortlisted.
- **Code of Conduct Compliance**: Adherence to the university placement policy, including acceptance of job offers, notice periods, and ethical conduct during corporate interactions.

### Additional Competency Requirements
- **Technical Competence**: Hands-on domain proficiency aligned with targeted job profiles.
- **Programming & Coding Knowledge**: Fluency in at least one modern programming language (C++, Java, Python, JavaScript).
- **Communication & Soft Skills**: Articulate verbal English communication, professional email writing, and interview etiquette.
- **Assessment Qualification**: Passing scores in company-administered aptitude tests, online coding rounds, and technical group discussions (GD).

## 3. Placement Preparation Framework

### Technical Preparation
- **Core Computer Science Fundamentals**:
  - **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)**: Arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, sorting, searching, hashing, dynamic programming, and algorithm complexity ($O(n)$ analysis).
  - **Database Management Systems (DBMS)**: SQL queries, relational schemas, ER modeling, normalization, indexing, transactions, and ACID properties.
  - **Operating Systems (OS)**: Process management, threads, concurrency, CPU scheduling, synchronization, deadlocks, and virtual memory paging.
  - **Computer Networks (CN)**: OSI model, TCP/IP stack, routing protocols, HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, and socket programming basics.
  - **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)**: Inheritance, encapsulation, polymorphism, abstraction, and design patterns in Java, C++, or Python.
  - **Software Engineering**: Agile methodologies, Git version control, CI/CD basics, unit testing, and software design lifecycles.
- **Role-Specific Advanced Technical Tracks**:
  - **AI / Machine Learning Roles**: Supervised & unsupervised learning, deep neural networks, model evaluation metrics, feature engineering, and PyTorch / TensorFlow / Scikit-learn.
  - **Web & Full-Stack Development Roles**: Front-end technologies (HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript/TypeScript, React/Vue), back-end frameworks (Node.js, Django, FastAPI, Spring Boot), and RESTful APIs.

### Aptitude & Analytical Preparation
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Arithmetic (percentages, ratios, profit & loss, time & work, speed & distance), algebra, geometry, permutations & combinations, and probability.
- **Logical Reasoning**: Puzzles, blood relations, syllogisms, seating arrangements, coding-decoding, and series completion.
- **Verbal Ability**: Reading comprehension, sentence correction, grammar rules, vocabulary, para-jumbles, and critical reasoning.
- **Data Interpretation**: Bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, tabular analysis, and caselets.
- **Problem-Solving Skills**: Structured analytical thinking and real-time business problem estimation (guesstimates).

### Interview Preparation & Soft Skills
- **Technical Interview Rounds**: In-depth code walkthroughs, data structure problem solving on whiteboard/live editor, and computer science fundamental questions.
- **HR & Behavioral Rounds**: Questions on strengths, areas of improvement, teamwork scenarios, conflict resolution, situational judgment, and long-term career aspirations.
- **Resume & Project Explanation**: Articulating problem statements, tech stack choices, individual contributions, challenges overcome, and business impact for every project listed on the resume.
- **Internship Discussion**: Detailed explanation of responsibilities, technical learning, and accomplishments during past internships.
- **Company-Specific Research**: Understanding recruiter history, core products, company values, recent press releases, and asking intelligent questions to interviewers.

## 4. Student Career Profile Blueprint
Students maintain a comprehensive university career profile reflecting their professional capabilities:
- **Demographic & Academic Data**: Name, university enrollment number, school, degree program, branch/specialization, current semester, cumulative CGPA, and graduation year.
- **Core Technical Skills**: Programming languages, database engines, web development stacks, AI/ML libraries, Cloud & DevOps platforms (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS/GCP).
- **Professional Skills**: Interpersonal communication, professional presentations, team collaboration, and leadership acumen.
- **Practical Experience**: Capstone projects, hackathon achievements, open-source contributions, research publications, industrial internships, and leadership roles in student councils or technical clubs.
- **Target Career Trajectories**: Software Development Engineer (SDE), Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, AI Engineer, Full-Stack Web Developer, Business Analyst, Product Manager, and Cybersecurity Analyst.
