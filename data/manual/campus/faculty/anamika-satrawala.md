# Faculty Profile: Dr. Anamika Satrawala

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Anamika Satrawala
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Computer Science and Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/anamika-satrawala
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: C-Programming Object Oriented Programming
- **Research Interests**: Internet of Vehicles Trust Management in Internet of Things Machine Learning Deep Learning
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Anamika Satrawala holds a PhD from Malaviya National Institute of Technology, Jaipur, in the area of Computer Science and Engineering with focus on Internet of Vehicles. In her academic career spanning more than 3 years, she has taught C programming, Object Oriented Programming, Web Application Development, Signals and Systems and Operating Systems. Her research is focussed mainly in the domain of Internet of Things. Her research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals. She is currently working in the Institute of Engineering and Technology, JK Lakshmipat University, Jaipur.
