- **Biography & Academic Background**: Amitanshu Shrivastava is an Assistant Professor at the JKLU Institute of Design, with over a decade of expertise in product design, creative storytelling, and education. He is passionate about merging traditional Indian crafts with modern design principles to create innovative and engaging solutions. A significant focus of his work is the design and development of educational games and puzzles, which inspire curiosity, foster learning, and encourage creative problem-solving among both children and adults. As the founder of Ubiquitous Objects , Amitanshu has led diverse projects that celebrate cultural heritage, innovation, and play. His work often involves creating meaningful experiences that bridge the gap between design and education, making learning an engaging and enjoyable journey. Through his games and puzzles, he seeks to simplify complex concepts, promote critical thinking, and connect learners with the joy of discovery. In his academic role, Amitanshu is deeply committed to mentoring future designers, helping them explore the intersections of culture, creativity, and functionality. He believes in the transformative power of design to solve real-world problems and create impact.
