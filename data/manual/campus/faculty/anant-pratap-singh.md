- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Anant Pratap Singh completed his doctoral degree from the Department of Mathematical Sciences, IIT (BHU). He has worked at IIT Delhi as a postdoctoral researcher in the area of Probabilistic Machine Learning. He holds his masters from NIT Jamshedpur and undergraduate from Ramjas College, University of Delhi. He has received prestigious DST-Inspire fellowship from Govt. of India and holds a gold medal in M.Sc. He has given several research presentations both at international and national level. He has visited Shanghai University, Shanghai, China for research collaborations and invited talks. He has worked in collaboration with several foreign researchers and scientists. His current area of research lies in Numerical computation, Statistical inference, Stochastic PDEs, Fractional calculus, etc.
