# Faculty Profile: Azhar Ahmed Sheikh

- **Faculty Name**: Azhar Ahmed Sheikh
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Institute of Management
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/azhar-ahmed-sheikh
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Banking Securities and Collection Operations Banking and Insurance Operations Introduction to Banking and Fintech
- **Research Interests**: Financial Statement Analysis Sustainable Finance Earnings Management AI & BFSI
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr Azhar A. Sheikh holds a PhD from Mohanlal Sukhadia University, Udaipur (Raj), in the area of commerce with a focus on financial statement analysis. Over a decade of experience in academia, he has taught Cost Accounting, Corporate Accounting, Business Statistics, Banking & Fintech courses at reputed Business schools in India. His research expertise is anchored around Financial Statement Analysis, Sustainable Finance, and Earnings Management. His research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals.
