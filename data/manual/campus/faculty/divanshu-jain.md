# Faculty Profile: Divanshu Jain

- **Faculty Name**: Divanshu Jain
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Electronics & Communication Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/divanshu-jain
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Fundamental of Automation Engineering Fundamental of Electrical and Electronics Engineering Digital Circuits Internet of Things Computer Architecture and Organization Digital Communication Digital Signal Processing Automation Project
- **Research Interests**: Internet of Things Antenna Designing Wireless Communications Computer Architecture
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Mr. Divanshu Jain is currently working as Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering. He did his M.E. in Communication Engineering from BITS Pilani, Pilani Campus in 2011, and B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Rajasthan University in 2008. He has over 14 years of academic experience. He is pursuing his Ph.D. in the area of Antenna Designing and Internet of Things. He has published several research papers in refereed International/National journals and conference proceedings. He has four patents published by Government of India. At JKLU, He has worked in various academic and non-academic committees such as IET Result Moderation committee, Alumni Coordinator, IET student farewell Committee, Fraternity Club, EEE Curriculum Committee, Community Development Committee etc. Earlier, He has taken the students to various industry visits such as, Maruti Suzuki India Limited, Gurgaon, Aksh OptiFibre Ltd, Reengus, Advanced Level Telecom Training Centre (ALTTC), Noida, Himachal Futuristic Communications Ltd. (HFCL), Goa, Neosol Gurgaon etc. He is working on various ranking platforms for JKLU such as NAAC, NIRF, THE ranking, India Today, Career360 etc. He has appointed as paper setter at various reputed universities. He is also very much devoted to Community Services; he was active member from 2013 to 2021 for Community services through JKLU Community Development Club. He is also awarded from Jaipur Nagar Nigam Heritage in year 2021. Also at the time of COVID-19, He was serving for the society through Elder Care Helpline and Samarth Foundation. It was a life mesmerizing experience to serve the society in the tough situation. He is also helping the school students or nurturing them in project making through JKLU ATAL Incubation centre, JKLU. He was also the project evaluator in Toycathon-2021 organised by Ministry of Education’s Innovation Cell and AICTE. Read More
