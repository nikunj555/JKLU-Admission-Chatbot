- **Biography & Academic Background**: Yash is a design professional with over 7 years of experience in the corporate and public sectors, coupled with 5+ years in design academia. Throughout his career, he has collaborated with prominent organizations, including the Government of Rajasthan and the Government of Bihar, on various grassroots-level awareness campaigns and public sector projects. These initiatives encompassed advertising, media management, and procurement. Yash's expertise lies in data-driven design, information visualization, campaign design, and management.
