# Faculty Profile: Dr. Lokanath Mishra

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Lokanath Mishra
- **Designation**: Professor
- **Department**: Finance and Accounts
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/lokanath-mishra
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Financial Management Advance Financial Management in Power BI Management Accounting Introduction to FinTech Derivative and Forex Business Simulation Business Law and Ethics
- **Research Interests**: Corporate Disclosure Practices Corporate Social Responsibility Corporate Governance Fintech and Credit Risk Management
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Lokanath Mishra is an avid learner and passionate teacher. He is dedicated to inspiring his students to discover their purpose and true potential. Lokanath Mishra has the opportunity to impact the lives of students and takes this role seriously. He has over 18 years of academic experience of teaching postgraduate and Graduate students in Accounting, Financial management, Derivative, Security Analysis and Portfolio Management, Forex Management and Business Law. ​ He is certified data analytics from IIM(K), Certified Biz Lab trainer from AIMA and on FICO Module from SAP. He is a Ph.D. from Central University of Tripura in corporate disclosure and reporting practices of Indian Companies. His Research Interest Includes the area of Corporate Reporting Practices, Fintech application, Behavioral Finance, Corporate Governance, Corporate Social Responsibility and sustainability issues. He has presented and published research papers in national and international journals of repute of Scopus indexed J​journal with ABDC’s ‘B’ Category, to name a few “Journal of Public Affairs” of Wiley Publication, “International Journal of Finance & Economics” of Wiley Publication, “Journal of Sustainable Finance & Investment” of Taylor & Francis, “International Journal of Business Excellence” of Inder science Publication. He has published more than 20 papers in reputed Journals and participated in many national and international Conference. He is also the recipient of Best Research paper award in the area of “Corporate Social Responsibility” from Delhi School of Economics, Delhi University at the annual Commerce Convention, 2014. ​He has conducted many executive training programs for the employees of JK Tyres, JK Papers, JK Lakshmi Cement and schoolteachers training program at MPS, Jaipur.
