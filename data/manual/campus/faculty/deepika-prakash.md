# Faculty Profile: Dr. Deepika Prakash

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Deepika Prakash
- **Designation**: Associate Professor
- **Department**: Computer Science and Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/deepika-prakash
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Information verified via official faculty directory
- **Research Interests**: Decision Support Systems Big Data Warehouses and Lakes Conceptual Design of IoT Systems AI and Health
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Deepika Prakash is currently Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at JK Lakshmipat University, Jaipur. She has done her M.Tech . from IIIT Bangalore and Ph.D , in Data Warehouse Systems from Delhi Technological University (formerly Delhi College of Engineering). She has over 10 years Industrial and Academic experience. In industry, Dr. Prakash worked in Motherson Sumi Infotech and Designs Ltd where she developed data marts in several areas She has worked in Big Data and has experience in the Hadoop eco system. Besides academic teaching, she has delivered long-term training programs to industrial professionals. As part of her research, Dr. Prakash is the co-author of two books published by Springer and IGI Global. She has published extensively and is on the Program Committee of several National and prestigious International Conferences notably ENASE. She also serves as a reviewer of various SCI journals.
