# Faculty Profile: Dr. Rabia Kamra

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Rabia Kamra
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Mathematics
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/rabia-kamra
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Calculus Matrix Computations Computational Data Analysis Linear Algebra and Differential Equations
- **Research Interests**: Numerical Linear Algebra Parallel Computing Numerical Analysis
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Rabia Kamra holds a PhD in Mathematics from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi, specializing in Numerical Analysis and Parallel Computing. Her research focuses on developing parallel algorithms for solving large-scale linear systems with special structures and properties. She has published high-quality research papers in internationally renowned journals. With over six years of teaching experience, she has served as an Assistant Professor at Punjab Engineering College (Deemed to be University), Chandigarh, NIT Delhi and currently contributes to the Institute of Engineering and Technology at JK Lakshmipat University, Jaipur
