# Faculty Profile: Dr. Hanuman Prasad Agrawal

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Hanuman Prasad Agrawal
- **Designation**: Associate Professor
- **Department**: Electrical and Electronic Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/hanuman-prasad-agrawal
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Electronic Devices and Circuits Industrial Drive and E-Vehicles Industrial Automation
- **Research Interests**: Power System Stability Power Quality Electric Vehicle Technology Embedded System and IoT
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Hanuman Prasad Agrawal serves as an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at JK Lakshmipat University. He earned his PhD from Jagannath University, Jaipur, and his MTech from Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT), Jaipur. With over 23 years of experience in teaching and administration across various engineering institutions, Dr. Agrawal has held key positions such as Head of Department, Controller of Examinations, Coordinator of the MTech Program, and Vice Principal at Sobhasaria Engineering College, Sikar, and Shekhawati Engineering College, Dundlod. He has guided numerous MTech students in their thesis work and has been instrumental in designing and organizing Faculty Development Programs, workshops, and seminars. Dr. Agrawal is a highly motivated member of JKLU faculty, seeking constant improvement in teaching and research methodology.
