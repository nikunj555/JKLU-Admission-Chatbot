# Faculty Profile: Dr. Riya Sureka

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Riya Sureka
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Finance and Accounts
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/riya-sureka
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Financial Accounting Indian Financial System Taxation Securities and Investment Analysis Research Methodology
- **Research Interests**: Behavioral Finance Behavioral Finance Behavioral Finance Bibliometric analysis and Systematic Literature Review
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Riya Sureka holds a Ph.D. from Malaviya National Institute of Technology, Jaipur, in the area of Behavioral Finance and Financial Technology. In her academic career spanning several years, she has taught Financial Accounting, Financial Markets, Financial Systems, and Investments at leading institutions. Her research expertise is centered on Behavioral Finance, Financial Technology, and Bibliometric Analysis. Her research contributions have been presented at prominent national and international conferences and published in leading ABDC A and B-category journals. Dr. Sureka's work has been recognized in the prestigious ABDC 2019 Author Rankings and P-Ranking 2020, and she has received accolades such as the UGC Junior Research Fellowship and the Venus International Women Researcher Award. She is currently an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Management, J.K. Lakshmipat University, Rajasthan.
