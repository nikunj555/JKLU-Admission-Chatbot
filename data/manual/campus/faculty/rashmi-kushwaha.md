# Faculty Profile: Dr. Rashmi Kushwaha

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Rashmi Kushwaha
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Electronics & Communication Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/rashmi-kushwaha
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Basic Electronics Peer-to-Peer Networks
- **Research Interests**: Peer-to-Peer Networks Distributed Systems Smart Grid Blockchain and its applications
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Rashmi Kushwaha holds a PhD from Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur, in the area of Peer-to-Peer Network, Distributed Systems and Blockchain. She has taught subjects like Basic Electronics, Data Communication & Networks and Peer-to-Peer Networks. Her research expertise is anchored around Peer-to-Peer video streaming, Blockchain, Distributed Systems, Smart Grid and Game Theory. Her research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences. She has also published various patents.
