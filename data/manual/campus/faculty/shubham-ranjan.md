# Faculty Profile: Dr. Shubham Ranjan

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Shubham Ranjan
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Institute of Management
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Management
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/shubham-ranjan
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Microeconomics MS Excel Fundamentals of Business Management
- **Research Interests**: Health Economics Gender Studies Public Health
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Shubham Ranjan is an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Management, JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU), Jaipur. He completed his Ph.D. at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mandi, with a doctoral thesis titled “Intersection of Gender and Diabetes in India: Determinants and Consequences.” His research examines how socio-economic, psychological, and physical health factors influence life satisfaction among diabetes patients in India. His academic interests lie in health economics, gender studies, and public health, with a focus on understanding social and economic determinants of well-being. Shubham teaches courses in economics, business statistics, and data analysis, integrating real-world applications with analytical and software-based learning. He actively incorporates tools such as MS Excel, Stata, RStudio, and SPSS to strengthen students’ quantitative and decision-making abilities. Currently, his research extends to developing a Health Insurance Equity Index (HIEI) to evaluate disparities in healthcare access, utilization, and financial protection across India. He is passionate about evidence-based policymaking and experiential teaching, and strives to bridge research and practice to promote inclusive and sustainable development.
