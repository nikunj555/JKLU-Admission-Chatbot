# Faculty Profile: Dr. Jaya Gupta

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Jaya Gupta
- **Designation**: Associate Professor
- **Department**: Mathematics
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/jaya-gupta
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Advanced Statistics Discrete Mathematics Optimization Calculus Differential Equations
- **Research Interests**: Special Functions Statistical Distribution Theory and Data Analysis Integral Transforms Fractional Calculus
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Jaya Gupta earned her Ph.D. from the University of Rajasthan, Jaipur, specializing in Special Functions and Integral Transforms. With nearly two decades of academic experience, she has taught a wide range of subjects, including Calculus, Integral Transforms, Discrete Mathematics, Optimization for Computer Science, Computational Engineering Analysis, Advanced Statistics, and Research Methodology, at prestigious institutes and universities across India. Dr. Gupta excels in interdisciplinary collaboration, working closely with faculty from mechanical, electrical, and computer science departments to integrate mathematics with engineering and technology. She has been deeply involved in implementing Project-Based Learning in mathematics courses, enhancing student engagement and practical understanding. She has published numerous research papers in peer-reviewed journals, presented her work at prestigious conferences, and delivered many invited lectures, reflecting her active engagement with the academic and research community. In addition to her teaching contributions, Dr. Jaya Gupta has guided a Ph.D. scholar and supervised numerous undergraduate student projects, highlighting her commitment to academic guidance and research excellence.
