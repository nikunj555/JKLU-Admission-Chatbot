# Faculty Profile: Dr. Rajnish Kumar

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Rajnish Kumar
- **Designation**: Assistant Professor
- **Department**: Electronics & Communication Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/rajnish-kumar
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Electronics Electrical Engineering Signals and systems Digital signal processing Electromagnetic field theory Antenna Digital communication
- **Research Interests**: Satellite and Wireless communication Machine learning Signal processing Quantum computing
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr Rajnish Kumar holds a PhD from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev, Israel in the area of Electrical and Computer Engineering with a focus on Wireless and Satellite Communication. In his academic career spanning 7 years, he has taught Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Signals and Systems, Digital Signal Processing, Electromagnetic Field Theory, Circuit Theory and Antennas at Banasthali University and K L University in India. His research expertise is anchored around Wireless communication, signal processing, machine learning and quantum computing. His research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals.
