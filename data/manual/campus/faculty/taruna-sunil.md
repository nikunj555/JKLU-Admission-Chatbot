# Faculty Profile: Dr. Taruna Sunil

- **Faculty Name**: Dr. Taruna Sunil
- **Designation**: Professor
- **Department**: Computer Science and Engineering
- **Institute / School**: Institute of Engineering & Technology
- **Official Profile URL**: https://jklu.edu.in/faculty/taruna-sunil
- **Verified Teaching Areas & Courses**: Blockchain Technology and Applications Computer Networks Database Management Systems Information Retrieval and Data Mining Design and Analysis of Algorithms Operating Systems
- **Research Interests**: Computer Network Blockchain Machine Learning Cloud Network
- **Biography & Academic Background**: Dr. Taruna Sunil holds a PhD from Banasthali Vidyapith, in the area of Computer Networks with focus on Wireless Sensor Networks. Her research expertise is anchored around Computer Network, Cloud computing and Machine Learning. Her research contributions have been presented in leading national as well as international conferences and published in renowned journals. Five students have completed their PhD under her mentorship. She is currently the Coordinator of B.Tech CSE program at JK Lakshmipat University.
