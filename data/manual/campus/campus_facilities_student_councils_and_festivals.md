# JKLU Campus Facilities, Student Councils and Annual Festivals

Official directory of campus infrastructure, student leadership councils, and annual university festivals at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU), Jaipur.

## Campus Facilities & Infrastructure
- **Advanced Labs**: Specialized state-of-the-art laboratories for computer science, artificial intelligence, robotics, data science, electronics, mechanical fabrication, and high-performance computing.
- **Design Studios**: Dedicated creative studios and individual workstations for students of the Institute of Design, supporting product design, interaction design, and visual communication.
- **Makerspace**: Collaborative hands-on innovation and prototyping facility equipped with 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC machines, electronics workstations, and rapid fabrication tooling.
- **Learning Resource Centre (LRC)**: The central university library housing an extensive physical and digital collection of textbooks, research journals, reference books, e-resources, discussion pods, and quiet study halls.
- **MP Ranjan Design Resource Centre**: A premier design repository and memorial archive honoring the legendary design educator Prof. M.P. Ranjan, featuring a curated collection of classic design volumes, research monographs, and design artifacts.
- **Hostels**: Modern, secure residential facilities on campus offering air-conditioned (AC) and non-AC options across single-bed, premium double-bed (attached washroom), double-sharing, and quad-sharing configurations.
- **Eateries**: Comprehensive campus food ecosystem including the multi-cuisine student mess, central cafeteria, food court counters, cafes, and late-evening snack kiosks.
- **Fitness Centre**: Modern air-conditioned gymnasium equipped with cardio treadmills, cross-trainers, free weights, resistance machines, and dedicated areas for yoga and aerobics.

## Student Councils (Student Governance)
Student leadership, campus activities, and university governance at JKLU are spearheaded by six primary student councils:
1. **Academic Affairs Council**: Represents student academic interests, facilitates student-faculty dialogues, coordinates academic grievance resolution, peer tutoring, and curriculum feedback mechanisms.
2. **Campus Life Council**: Oversees student residential welfare, hostel amenities, dining and mess quality oversight, campus sustainability, and day-to-day student living conditions.
3. **Cultural Affairs Council**: Promotes artistic expression, theatre, music, dance, visual arts, and coordinates the university's flagship annual cultural festival, Sabrang.
4. **Technical Council**: Fuels technical innovation, robotics clubs, competitive programming, AI/ML study groups, hackathons (including HackJKLU), and developer workshops across campus.
5. **Sports Council**: Governs all university sports teams, intramural tournaments, athletic training infrastructure, and organizes the national inter-university sports festival, Spardha.
6. **Public Relations Council (PR Council)**: Manages university student media, social media channels, event photo/video coverage, campus publications, external communications, and student outreach.

## University Festivals & Annual Flagship Events
- **Spardha**: The flagship annual national inter-university sports festival organized by the Sports Council, hosting collegiate teams from across India in cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, chess, and athletics.
- **Sabrang**: The premier 3-day annual techno-cultural festival of JK Lakshmipat University, featuring celebrity pronites, inter-college dance and musical competitions, drama, fashion shows, esports, and creative arts.
- **Aarambh**: The official university freshers' induction program, orientation festival, and student club onboarding carnival designed to welcome incoming undergraduate and postgraduate students to university life.
- **Hack JKLU (HackJKLU)**: The premier annual 24–36 hour national hackathon attracting student developers, designers, and innovators nationwide to solve real-world problems in AI/ML, Web3, FinTech, and IoT with industry mentorship and cash prizes.
- **Jaipur Design Week (JDW)**: The flagship design festival organized by the Institute of Design, featuring design exhibits, international keynote speakers, masterclasses, and portfolio showcases.
- **Design Guru Day**: An annual institutional tradition celebrating seminal contributions to Indian and global design, highlighted by the prestigious Design Guru Award conferment.
- **Laureate Awards**: The prestigious annual university honours convocation recognizing outstanding student achievements in academics, leadership, sports, community service, and extracurricular excellence.
- **Parichay**: An informal talent showcase and ice-breaking gathering where first-year students interact with senior peers, faculty mentors, and campus clubs.
- **Sangam**: The annual university alumni confluence and homecoming reunion connecting past graduates with current students, faculty, and industry leaders.
- **Aarohan**: The introductory cultural and performing arts launchpad event where incoming freshers demonstrate their talents in music, dance, and dramatic arts.
