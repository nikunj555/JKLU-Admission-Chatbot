# Aarambh '26 Youth Festival — Rules, Registration and Code of Conduct

Official policies, participation regulations, fees, code of conduct, and legal terms for Aarambh '26 at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU).

## 1. Registration & Entry Requirements
- **Eligibility & Requirements**: Participation in Aarambh '26 requires:
  1. Completion of the official event registration form.
  2. Payment of the mandatory registration fee of **rupees 2500** (INR 2,500).
  3. A valid university student identity card.
- **Entry Protocol**: Entry into the festival premises will strictly and only be granted upon successful barcode/QR code scans at the check-in terminal.

## 2. Strict Code of Conduct
- All registrants must adhere strictly to the high disciplinary standards of JK Lakshmipat University.
- **Zero Tolerance Policy**: Any form of harassment, destruction of property, disruption of events, or usage of banned substances is strictly prohibited.
- **Consequences**: Any violation will result in immediate disqualification and removal from campus without refund.

## 3. Payment & Non-Refundability Policy
- **Registration Fee**: The registration fee is **rupees 2500** (INR 2,500).
- **Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable**: The registration fee of rupees 2500 is completely non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances, as detailed in our Refund Policy.
- **Gateway Convenience Fee**: A 2% gateway transaction convenience fee charged by Cashfree Payments is applicable and must be paid by the participant during checkout.
- **Duplicate Charges**: Duplicate charges arising from network delays during transaction processing will be verified and reconciled manually by our team upon submission of proof.

## 4. Media & Publicity Release
- By participating in Aarambh '26, participants grant the organizing committee and JK Lakshmipat University the absolute right and permission to take photographs, audio recordings, or videos during the festival and utilize them for institutional promotions, publications, and web marketing.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
- The organizing committee, volunteers, and JK Lakshmipat University shall not be held liable for any loss, damage, theft of personal belongings, or physical injury sustained by participants during the events of the youth festival.
