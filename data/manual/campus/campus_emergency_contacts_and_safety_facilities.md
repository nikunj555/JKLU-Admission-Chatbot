# JKLU Campus Safety: Emergency Contacts, Protocols & Safety Facilities

Official university safety directory outlining emergency escalation contacts, security personnel, fire safety systems, and medical emergency facilities at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU), Jaipur.

## 1. Campus Emergency Contacts & Support Directory
In the event of an urgent security, medical, infrastructural, or personal emergency, students and staff should immediately reach out to the designated university support offices:
- **Campus Security Control Room**: 24/7 security control room and main campus gate command center for emergency response, visitor screening, and immediate security personnel dispatch.
- **Medical & First-Aid Support Centre**: 24/7 on-campus health clinic providing emergency medical assistance, doctor consultations, first aid, and ambulance dispatch.
  - Primary Health Clinic Contact / Emergency Helpline: Available 24/7 on campus.
  - Mental Health Counselling (Mindery Helpline): **+91 91661 44120** (24/7 online; in-person Thursdays 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
- **Hostel Wardens & Residence Advisors**: Round-the-clock emergency assistance for residential students regarding sudden illness, room emergencies, or safety concerns.
- **Campus Transport & Ambulance Support**: Dedicated emergency transport and 24/7 on-campus ambulance service operated in partnership with **Blue Circle Medi Services Pvt. Ltd.** for rapid transit to Jaipur referral hospitals.
- **Maintenance & Engineering Emergency Department**: Rapid response electrical, plumbing, generator, and civil maintenance for safety hazards, short circuits, or structural issues.
- **University IT Emergency Helpdesk**: Immediate resolution for campus-wide network blackouts, critical server issues, or security breaches.
- **Office of Student Affairs & Dean of Students**: Institutional leadership for pastoral student support, crisis management, emergency travel permissions, and guardian communication.

## 2. Emergency Facilities & Campus Safety Infrastructure
JKLU maintains rigorous institutional safety infrastructure compliant with national safety norms:
- **Comprehensive First-Aid Facilities**: Fully stocked first-aid response kits stationed in all academic laboratories, engineering workshops, sports complexes, hostel warden offices, and cafeteria counters.
- **Fire Safety Equipment**: Advanced fire safety systems installed across all buildings, including smoke and heat detectors, manual call points, fire hose reels, wet risers, and ABC-type portable fire extinguishers checked and inspected regularly.
- **Clearly Marked Emergency Exits**: Photoluminescent exit signage and wide, unobstructed emergency fire escape staircases marked on every floor of all academic and residential buildings.
- **Trained Security Personnel**: Professional security guards on active duty 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, conducting regular perimeter patrols and vehicular entry/exit checks.
- **Electronic CCTV Monitoring**: High-definition CCTV camera network continuously monitoring campus main gates, building lobbies, parking zones, hostel perimeters, and common corridors.
- **Emergency Public Communication & Alert System**: Centralized public address systems and rapid SMS/portal broadcast mechanisms to notify the campus community during emergency drills or weather advisories.
