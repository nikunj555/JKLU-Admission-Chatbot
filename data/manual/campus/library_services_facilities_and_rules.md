# JKLU Library & Learning Resource Centre (LRC): Services, Collections & Regulations

Comprehensive operational guide to the library collections, electronic databases, student reading spaces, and institutional borrowing regulations at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU).

## 1. Library Collections & Services
The Learning Resource Centre (LRC) serves as the intellectual cornerstone of JKLU, housing extensive physical and electronic academic assets:
- **Comprehensive Textbooks Collection**: Multiple copies of all prescribed and recommended textbooks for engineering, management, design, and humanities courses across all semesters.
- **Authoritative Reference Books**: Deep reference section containing classic treatises, handbooks, technical manuals, dictionaries, encyclopedias, and rare design monographs (including the MP Ranjan Design Archive).
- **Peer-Reviewed Research Journals**: Subscriptions to leading national and international peer-reviewed academic journals, conference proceedings, and periodicals.
- **Expansive E-Books Library**: Access to tens of thousands of electronic books accessible remotely through the library portal for 24/7 academic study.
- **Digital Databases & Online Repositories**: Direct institutional access to world-class research repositories including IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, ScienceDirect, J-GATE, EBSCO, and DELNET.
- **Newspapers & Periodicals Section**: Daily regional and national broadsheets, business dailies (The Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard), and weekly tech and cultural magazines.
- **Dedicated Reading & Study Zones**: Spacious, naturally lit, climate-controlled reading halls with ergonomic study desks, individual carrels, and collaborative discussion zones.
- **Computer & Digital Terminals**: Dedicated computer workstations equipped with high-speed internet for online catalog searching (OPAC), electronic document retrieval, and digital research.

## 2. Library Regulations & Borrowing Rules
To maintain an orderly, scholarly environment, all library patrons must adhere to official LRC rules:
- **Mandatory University Student ID**: A valid, non-transferable JKLU student identity card is mandatory for library entry, electronic check-in, and all book issue/return transactions.
- **Loan Period & Borrowing Limits**: Borrowed books must be returned or renewed on or before the due date specified at the time of issue (standard borrowing window: 14 days for undergraduate students, with renewal options if not reserved by others).
- **Strict Maintenance of Silence**: Complete silence must be maintained in all designated reading halls and individual study zones. Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode; loud talking or phone conversations inside reading halls are strictly prohibited.
- **Careful Handling of Library Resources**: Library books, journals, and digital assets must be handled with utmost care. Marking, underlining, dog-earing pages, writing annotations, or defacing library materials is strictly forbidden.
- **Late Return Fines & Penalties**: Overdue materials not returned within the designated borrowing period attract applicable per-day late return fines as per library policy. Persistent delinquency may lead to suspension of borrowing privileges.
- **Loss or Damage of Books**: In the event of book loss or physical mutilation, the borrower is required to replace the publication with an identical latest edition or pay the replacement cost plus administrative processing fees.
- **Adherence to Library Timings**: Students must observe official library opening and closing hours, including special extended hours during mid-term and end-term examinations.
