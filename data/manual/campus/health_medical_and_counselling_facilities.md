# JKLU Health Facilities, Medical Assistance and Mental Health Counselling

Officially listed healthcare and medical support infrastructure on the JK Lakshmipat University campus.

## 1. On-Campus Health Centre
- **Overview**: Dedicated university health centre providing medical assistance and clinical consultation.
- **24/7 Availability**: Medical assistance from qualified doctors and healthcare professionals is available around the clock (24 hours a day, 7 days a week).
- **Services Provided**: First aid, primary diagnostic assessment, emergency treatment, regular preventative health check-ups, and continuous follow-up services for campus residents and students.

## 2. Emergency Ambulance Service
- **24/7 Rapid Response**: JKLU lists a 24/7 emergency ambulance service stationed directly on campus.
- **Service Management**: Managed and operated by **Blue Circle Medi Services Pvt. Ltd.** to ensure swift medical transport and emergency transit to top partner hospitals in Jaipur whenever required.

## 3. Mental Health Counselling — Mindery
- **Overview**: Professional, confidential psychological counselling and mental health support provided through **Mindery**.
- **Scope**: Confidential guidance and therapy for students experiencing academic stress, emotional anxiety, exam pressures, depression, interpersonal concerns, and difficulties in adjusting to university life.
- **Online Counselling**: Available 24/7 for remote chats and virtual sessions.
- **In-Person Sessions**: Scheduled on-campus in-person counselling sessions are listed on **Thursdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**.
- **Counselling Helpline Contact**: **+91 91661 44120** (+91-9166144120).
