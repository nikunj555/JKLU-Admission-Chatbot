# JKLU Admission & Registration Document Checklist

Official checklist of required original documents and self-attested photocopies for new students joining JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU).

## 1. Personal Documents
- **Aadhaar Card or Accepted Government Photo ID**: Valid government-issued photo identity proof (Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
- **Passport-size Photographs**: Recent colored passport-size photographs of the candidate for official student records, file dossiers, and campus identity cards.
- **Date-of-Birth Proof**: Official Birth Certificate issued by the Municipal Corporation / Registrar of Births or Class 10 certificate indicating the candidate's exact date of birth (if separately required).
- **Active Contact Details**: Candidate's active and personal email ID and mobile phone number for university ERP portal access, SMS updates, academic communication, and official notices.

## 2. Academic Documents
- **Class 10 Marksheet**: Official Class X board examination marksheet.
- **Class 10 Passing Certificate**: Secondary School Passing Certificate issued by the examination board (CBSE, ICSE, or State Board).
- **Class 12 Marksheet**: Official Class XII qualifying examination marksheet verifying eligibility percentages.
- **Class 12 Passing Certificate**: Senior Secondary School Passing Certificate issued by the examination board.
- **Class 12 Provisional Certificate**: Provisional certificate issued by school authorities if the final passing certificate has not yet been disbursed by the board.
- **Class 12 Migration Certificate**: Official Migration Certificate from the concerned education board (CBSE, ICSE, or State Board), if required.
- **Transfer Certificate (TC)**: Original Transfer Certificate (TC) / School Leaving Certificate issued by the head of the last attended institution.
- **Entrance Examination Scorecard**: Official scorecard for entrance examinations (e.g. JEE Main, CUET, CLAT, UCEED, NATA, or JKLU Design/Tech Entrance Test), if applicable.

## 3. JKLU Admission Documents
- **JKLU Application Form / Confirmation**: Printed copy of the submitted online JKLU application form or official application confirmation acknowledgement.
- **Provisional Admission Offer Letter**: Official Provisional Admission Offer Letter issued by JK Lakshmipat University.
- **Admission Acceptance / Confirmation**: Signed admission acceptance slip confirming enrollment.
- **Admission Fee Payment Receipt**: Proof / bank transaction receipt of the admission fee and academic tuition fee payment.
- **Signed Admission Forms & Declarations**: Completed and signed student declarations, code of conduct undertakings, and parent/guardian declarations requested by JKLU.
- **Student Registration & Enrollment Details**: Official university registration and enrollment documentation issued upon physical reporting on campus.

## 4. Additional Documents (If Applicable)
- **Category Certificate**: Reserved category certificate (SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, or PwD) issued by an authorized government authority, if claiming any category or fee concession benefit.
- **Scholarship Application & Supporting Documents**: Merit scholarship application along with supporting grade transcripts, JEE Main rank card, or parent income certificates as specified under the JKLU Scholarship Policy.
- **Gap-Year Affidavit**: Notarized gap-year affidavit/certificate explaining academic break or gap years between Class 12 and university enrollment, if applicable.
- **Medical Fitness Certificate**: Medical fitness certificate from an authorized registered medical practitioner (MBBS or equivalent), certifying physical fitness and blood group.
- **Anti-Ragging Undertaking**: Mandatory anti-ragging affidavit/undertaking signed online (via UGC / Anti-Ragging portal) and submitted by both the student and parent.
- **Hostel Allotment Confirmation & Forms**: Signed hostel admission form, room allotment slip, hostel rule acceptance undertaking, and medical history declaration (if opting for campus residence).
