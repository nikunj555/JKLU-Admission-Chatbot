# JKLU Student Support Services: Academic Advising, Career Assistance & Grievance Redressal

Official institutional charter outlining student mentoring, academic tutorials, career acceleration services, and multi-tier student grievance redressal mechanisms at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU).

## 1. Academic Support & Student Mentoring
JKLU provides dedicated academic scaffolding to ensure every student succeeds in their degree journey:
- **Faculty Mentoring Program**: Every undergraduate and postgraduate student is assigned a designated faculty mentor who conducts regular one-on-one sessions to track academic progress, semester course selection, and holistic personal growth.
- **Academic Advising**: Departmental advisors provide customized guidance on elective choices, multidisciplinary minor pathways, academic credit overloads, and study-abroad credit transfers.
- **Structured Doubt-Solving Sessions**: Regularly scheduled interactive doubt-clearing sessions organized by course instructors following core lecture modules.
- **Tutorial & Remedial Classes**: Small-group tutorial classes designed to reinforce fundamental concepts, solve complex mathematical/algorithmic problem sets, and provide remedial help for students needing academic strengthening.
- **Academic & Career Counselling**: Dedicated educational counsellors to assist students with exam anxiety, study scheduling, goal-setting, and specialization selection.

---

## 2. Comprehensive Career Support & Employability Initiatives
Spearheaded by the university Placement & Training Cell:
- **End-to-End Placement Assistance**: Robust corporate outreach connecting graduating students with top tier multinationals, high-growth startups, and consulting firms across India.
- **Internship Assistance**: Structured facilitation for mandatory summer internships, semester-long industry immersions, and paid research internships.
- **Resume-Building Workshops**: Expert masterclasses on drafting ATS-friendly, standardized technical and managerial resumes highlighting impactful projects and verifiable metrics.
- **Mock Interviews & Feedback Rounds**: Rigorous simulated technical and HR interviews conducted by industry leaders and corporate alumni with structured feedback rubrics.
- **Aptitude & Analytical Training**: Comprehensive preparatory bootcamps covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal comprehension, and problem-solving speed tests.
- **Technical Workshops & Bootcamps**: Intensive hands-on workshops on trending technologies including Generative AI, Cloud Computing, Full-Stack Web Development, Data Engineering, and UI/UX Design.
- **Corporate & Industry Interaction Sessions**: Leadership talks, guest lecture series, fireside chats with founders, and company visits to expose students to corporate work cultures and future industry expectations.

## 3. Student Grievance Redressal Mechanisms
JKLU maintains a transparent, time-bound, multi-tier system to address student complaints and feedback:
- **Academic Grievances**: Fair and speedy resolution of concerns regarding course grading, attendance discrepancies, timetable clashes, syllabus coverage, or examination scheduling via the Departmental Academic Committee.
- **Hostel-Related Complaints**: Immediate redressal of room maintenance, housekeeping, water heating, plumbing, electrical, and mess/dining quality issues via the Hostel Warden and Student Campus Life Council.
- **Infrastructure & Maintenance Complaints**: Portal-based logging and tracking for repairs in classrooms, laboratories, sports grounds, and student common spaces.
- **IT & Wi-Fi Network Support**: Dedicated university IT Helpdesk resolving hostel/campus Wi-Fi connectivity problems, ERP student portal login issues, university email access, and lab computing accounts.
- **Library Grievances**: Formal channels to request new book acquisitions, resolve RFID card checkout errors, report database access issues, and address study hall concerns with library staff.
- **General Student Grievances & Welfare**: Dedicated University Student Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC) ensuring confidential, unbiased hearing of student disputes, administrative bottlenecks, and welfare matters.
