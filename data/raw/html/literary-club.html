Our Club is a community dedicated to fostering a love for literature, creative expression, and intellectual interaction. We aim to inspire curiosity, celebrate diverse narratives, and empower individuals to express themselves with confidence.

Whether you are an avid reader, a budding writer, or an enthusiastic speaker, the Literary Club welcomes you to be part of an enriching journey of literary exploration.

To promote literature, creativity, and community engagement and Inclusivity. The main vision was to introduce literary club to the students of JKLU, to let them know more about it.
