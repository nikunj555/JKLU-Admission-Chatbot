Professor

Computer Science and Engineering

Institute of Engineering & Technology

Biography

Dr. Amit Kumar Sinhal is currently working as a Professor and coordinator of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU). He has over 27 years of extensive experience in software development, project management, research, and academia.  He served as visiting Professor at Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT). Dr. Sinhal holds a PhD and M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Rajiv Gandhi Technical University and completed his Postdoctoral research at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette. Additionally, he is a Certified Master Trainer in High-Performance Computing (HPC) by AICTE-CDAC and hold professional certifications in Computer Science Education from IIIT Delhi and Project-Based Learning from Olin College of Engineering, USA.
