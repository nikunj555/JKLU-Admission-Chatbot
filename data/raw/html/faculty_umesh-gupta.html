Dr. Umesh Gupta

Professor and Dean of Academic Affairs

Mathematics

Institute of Engineering & Technology

Biography

Professor (Dr.) Umesh Gupta has 25 years of experience in teaching and research. He is working as Dean of Academic Affairs and Professor in Mathematics at JK Lakshmipat University, Jaipur. His areas of expertise are Statistical Data Analysis and Optimization Techniques. He had also worked as a Visiting Professor at St. Cloud State University, USA. He is a member of the American Mathematical Society, Indian Mathematical Society, Soft Computing Research Society, ISTD Jaipur, Vigyan Bharti Rajasthan, Operational Research Society of India, and the Rajasthan Academy of Physical Sciences. Umesh Gupta is a senior member of IACSIT, Singapore. He has a number of research publications in various International & National Journals & Conference Proceedings to his credit. Umesh Gupta has written the textbooks of Engineering Mathematics and Research Methods in Management. Umesh Gupta was the founder and president of the Institution’s Innovation Council of MHRD, Govt. of India at JKLU, and also the core team member of the Atal Incubation Center – JKLU. His research interest includes Statistical Data Analysis, Optimization Techniques, and Fluid Mechanics.
